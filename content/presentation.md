# Silta

A bridge to cancer care for people who do not speak Finnish.

Junction 2026 · AstraZeneca — Equal Care Across Finland

Note:
Open with one person: she noticed a lump three weeks ago and does not know whom to call.

---

## 1 in 9 people in Finland speak another language

- 646,392 people (11.4%) have a native language other than Finnish, Swedish or Sámi.
- They attend screening about **20 points less** often: breast 60.8% vs 83.2% (2024).
- The gap grew between 2020 and 2024.

---

## The front doors are in Finnish

- Omaolo, Maisa, Lunna and 116117: Finnish, Swedish, partly English; most need bank ID.
- Translated letters alone do not help (Norway: 46.5% vs 47.4%).
- Acting at the visit does (London: 56% took a self-sampling kit offered at a GP visit, vs ~13% by post).

---

## Silta follows one person

1. Find information in your own language
2. Describe what you noticed — voice, tap or write
3. The right door in your county, with a Finnish call script
4. A visit card and flash cards at the visit
5. What happens next, and when to call

---

## The clinician gets a Finnish summary

- One screen, read in 30 seconds.
- Preferences known in advance: interpreter language, comfort with a cervical sample.
- A prompt to offer a home self-sampling kit when screening is overdue.

---

## Safe by design

- Not a diagnosis: no urgency scores; emergency signs point to 112 and 116117.
- A random code instead of identity; it stops working after 90 days, and you can delete your data.
- Voice only where it works: Somali answers by cards, and human recordings replace generated audio.
