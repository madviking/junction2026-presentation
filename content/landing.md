## One journey, five steps

1. **Describe** what you noticed by tapping pictures, speaking or writing.
2. **Find the right door** in your county, with a Finnish call script to read aloud.
3. **At the visit**, show a visit card. The clinician gets a one-screen Finnish summary.
4. **After the visit**, see what happens next and when to call if nothing arrives.
5. **Screening letter?** Scan its QR code for a picture guide in your language.

Built for people with another native language first. The same picture cards, audio and no-ID start also serve people with low health literacy, older people, newcomers and the family members who help them.
