## The problem

Cancer screening in Finland works well for people who read Finnish or Swedish. For everyone else it works much less well: in 2024, people whose native language is not Finnish, Swedish or Sámi attended screening about 20 percentage points less often in every programme, and the gap is growing.

The digital front doors (Omaolo, Maisa, Lunna, 116117) work in Finnish, Swedish and partly English, and most need Finnish bank ID. Counties are moving first contact into chat-based digital clinics, and these reach fewer of the same people: in the eight counties of a 2026 register study, people whose native language is not Finnish or Swedish were 4.8% of residents but 3% of digital-clinic users. Translated letters alone do not change much: in a Norwegian trial, invitations in the language of origin gave 46.5% attendance against 47.4%. What works is acting at the visit: when an HPV self-sampling kit was offered during a GP visit for another reason, 56% took one, against about 13% response by post.

## What Silta does

Silta is a multilingual, mobile-first web app that follows one person from "I noticed something" to the right door and a visit both sides understand.

1. **Find** health-service information in your own language.
2. **Prepare.** Describe what you noticed by voice, tapping or writing. It is a checklist, not a diagnosis.
3. **Navigate.** For your county: who to call, what to expect, a Finnish call script to read aloud, and your right to a free professional interpreter.
4. **At the visit.** A visit card and flash cards that show your symptoms with very little Finnish.
5. **After the visit.** What happens next, when, and when to call if nothing arrives.
6. **For the clinician.** A one-screen Finnish summary, preferences known in advance, and a prompt to offer a home self-sampling kit when cervical screening is overdue.

Voice depends on how well speech AI works in each language. English, Finnish and Arabic get voice in and out. Somali gets audio out but answers by cards, because speech recognition is weak in Somali; a native speaker's recording replaces the generated audio once approved.

## Why it matters for the National Cancer Strategy

Goal 3 asks that patients start treatment within target times. The time from first symptom to first contact is the largest single part of the delay, and low health literacy lengthens it. Silta works on exactly those first steps. It also targets sub-goal 1.3: screening participation of people whose mother tongue is not Finnish or Swedish should reach the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers.

## Privacy and safety

- **Not a diagnostic tool.** No urgency rating of your symptoms; emergency signs always point to 112 and 116117.
- **A random code instead of identity.** No names, ID numbers, phone numbers or emails. A visit code stops working after 90 days, you can delete your data at any time, and analytics are anonymous.
- **Prepares the visit, does not replace an interpreter.** Silta reminds people of their right to a professional one.
