# Silta

**Helps language minorities in Finland act on possible cancer symptoms and use screening, in their own language.**

Junction 2026 · AstraZeneca — Equal Care Across Finland

## The gap

- 646,392 people (11.4%) in Finland speak a language other than Finnish, Swedish or Sámi.
- In 2024 they attended screening about 20 points less: breast 60.8% vs 83.2%, cervical 56.3% vs 76.2%, colorectal 53.4% vs 74.5%.
- Digital front doors are in Finnish, Swedish and partly English, and most need bank ID.

## What Silta does

1. **Find** information in your own language.
2. **Prepare:** describe what you noticed by voice, tap or text. A checklist, not a diagnosis.
3. **Navigate:** the right door in your county, a Finnish call script, your right to an interpreter.
4. **At the visit:** a visit card and flash cards.
5. **For the clinician:** a one-screen Finnish summary and a self-sampling prompt when screening is overdue.

## Safe by design

No diagnosis or urgency scores · emergency signs point to 112 / 116117 · a random code instead of identity, valid for 90 days · delete your data any time.
