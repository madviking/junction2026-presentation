What the numbers say: reach is not the problem a letter solves. A person acts when someone they trust asks them at the right moment and the next step is easy. So every channel below is judged on two things: does it reach people at a trigger moment, and does it hand them straight to an action.

Three ways in

Each way in has a different owner

Who controls the moment decides which channels work. The letter belongs to the county, the noticed symptom belongs to the person, and the visit preparation belongs to the care team.

Rollout

From the first users to national practice

Each phase produces the evidence the next one needs. Targets are proposals for the team to agree, not commitments.

P0 · Start now

Usage without anyone's permission

  • Two or three community organisations co-test the app and hand out printouts.
  • Prerendered answer pages per language × symptom × county, so search and AI assistants can read Silta.
  • Share link for helping a relative.
  • Small language-targeted ads test timed with screening-letter weeks.
  • Printouts in libraries, Finnish courses, places of worship, community centres.

Proposed gate: first 100 real journeys, at least 3 languages, a visit card shown at a real appointment, community partner quotes.

P1 · One hyvinvointialue

Helsinki or Vantaa–Kerava pilot

  • First move: nurses at 2–3 health stations send the prepare-for-your-visit link.
  • Clinician view used at those stations; doctors judge whether it saves time.
  • Waiting-room posters and A6 cards in the county's top languages.
  • QR and short link in screening invitations and SMS reminders.
  • Funding through roadmap action 1.3.1 (€400k, counties + HUS + NGOs, 2026–2028).

Proposed gate: share of interpreted visits prepared in Silta, nurse and doctor feedback, screening attendance in pilot areas vs comparison areas.

P2 · National

Recommended practice

  • QR block in THL's model screening invitation.
  • Links from Omaolo, Terveyskylä, InfoFinland and the 116117 phone line.
  • Pre-visit preparation as a recommended step for interpreted visits.
  • Part of the national symptom-awareness plan (roadmap 1.3.2).
  • Health-system lesson in integration training.

Goal it serves: National Cancer Strategy sub-goal 1.3: screening participation of non-Finnish/Swedish speakers reaches the level of Finnish and Swedish speakers.

Measurement without personal data

Every link and QR code carries a channel code

The code is counted once when a journey starts and stored only as an anonymous count by language and county, next to the counts the county view already shows. No cookies, no identity, no tracking across sessions.

Open questions

Decisions the team needs

  1. Domain and short link for QR codes and printouts.
  2. Which county we name as the pilot in the pitch: Helsinki (largest numbers, lowest attendance) or Vantaa–Kerava (highest foreign-language share, 28.4%).
  3. Who hosts Silta long term: county, Cancer Society, or a nonprofit. This decides Google Ad Grants eligibility and who signs data agreements.
  4. Which community organisations we contact first, and who on the team owns that.
  5. Printout languages for the first batch and who checks them (native speakers).
  6. Wording for the nurse-initiated way in: "prepare for your visit" (esitiedot), never "screening".
