The gap opens at the door

People whose native language is not Finnish, Swedish or Sámi get the same screening letter but attend about 20 points less often. After they attend, most of the gap closes. Most later steps are not reported by language at all. Select a step to see the evidence and what we propose.

Each value is the share of people who reached that step and took it (2024 unless noted). Pills show the gap in percentage points, in coral when the 95% confidence interval excludes zero. Boxed labels are national strategy targets.

How to read this

  • “Other native language” includes people whose native language is unknown. The registry does not split attendance by individual language.
  • Language, education and region of birth overlap. Across ~290 municipalities, language still predicts lower breast and colorectal attendance after controlling for education, age, urbanisation and unemployment; for cervical screening, unemployment explains the area differences. This is an area-level model and does not prove cause.
  • County waiting times were read from Cancer Registry charts (±0.1 month).
  • Sources: Finnish Cancer Registry mass-screening statistics, published 20 May 2026 (native language, region of birth, education, previous result, delays); National Cancer Strategy 2026–2035; THL Maamu 2010–12 and FinMonik 2018–19 surveys; Norway RCT; YouScreen (London); Community Guide.
