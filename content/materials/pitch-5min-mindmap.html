Q&A in 20 seconds

  1. Language, not education? Not claimed as the cause. After controls, language still predicts lower breast and colorectal attendance; cervical tracks unemployment. Africa-born women: −26% after adjustment. Mea
  2. Letters are already translated. Norway 46.5 vs 47.4. Silta adds the room, her rights, rebooking and the Finnish words. The pilot measures the effect. Timo
  3. What does it cost the county? One printed block plus posters; no IT. A pilot isn't priced, and we won't guess. €400k (1.3.1); €121M a year reaches Helsinki via the foreign-language criterion. Timo
  4. Goal 3? Not split by language anywhere. Screening = sub-goal 1.3, upstream. 2nd gap: colonoscopy after a positive test 68% vs 77% (2022–24), the primary-to-specialist step. Mea
  5. Who checked the Somali? Machine-translated, marked unchecked. Native-speaker + clinical review = pilot task 1. Sources + checked dates; changes logged, can be rolled back. Milla
  6. Medical device? GDPR? AI? No diagnosis or urgency rating (MDR Rule 11); 112 on every screen. No names or IDs; hashed code, 90 days. AI suggests, the person confirms; the route never comes from the model.
  7. Helsinki isn't a wellbeing services county. Right: it organises its own services. 147,482 foreign-language residents (21.2%). Länsi-Uusimaa works the same and is already mapped. Timo
Bonus: "What do you need from us?"
  • An introduction to the screening lead in Helsinki / HUS, and a medical-affairs reviewer. We bring the working product.
