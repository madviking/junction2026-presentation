Three ways in, and we can only see one

People reach cancer care through a screening letter, through a symptom they notice, or because a doctor orders a test. Only the screening route is measured by native language, and that is where the largest gap is. Select a step on the map.

How to read this, limits and sources
  • “Other native language” includes people whose native language is unknown. The registry does not split attendance by individual language.
  • The three screening programmes are combined on the map. Gaps are within 2 points of each other at attendance, so one figure stands for all three; select a step for each programme.
  • The symptom-route fork comes from one Finnish study of diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (160 patients, 2014–2020). It groups people by where they first sought care, not by language.
