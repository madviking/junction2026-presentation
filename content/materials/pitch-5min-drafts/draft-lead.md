# Lead draft (Opus 5.5, main session) — position before seeing the others

Message: "The cancer screening gap for people with another native language opens at the letter, before anyone in care sees them. Silta closes that step today, with no IT project, and a county can measure it with a table it already gets."
Ask: Helsinki pilot — QR strip on one cervical invitation batch, compare attendance per 100 invitees by native language vs a comparison batch. AZ as convenor with the county.

Positions:
1. One story, not two. Hodan (screening) carries the talk. Olga (symptom) = one sentence + one screenshot, or cut. The symptom route has no language data; leading with it costs Data-transparency points.
2. The gap must be LOCATED, not just sized (Insight 25%): invitation → attendance is where it opens; once in care, follow-up is equal (colposcopy 84.8 vs 87.1). That one contrast is the insight. Say it in one breath.
3. Live demo = physical letter + phone scan of its QR → Somali guide → press play (voice) → Finnish phrase to ask for a female sample-taker / interpreter. That is the wow; ~60–75 s. Do it on a real phone mirrored, or on /try iframe as fallback. Screens on the morph slide are the second fallback.
4. Actionability (30%) gets its own 45 s: three steps a county can do Monday (0 / 1 QR / 2 posters), the metric (registry attendance by native language), the money already exists (€400k action 1.3.1 in roadmap).
5. One sentence of "what we don't claim" earns Data-quality points cheaply. Say it, don't show a slide.
6. Don't click drills or the pathway embed live. Drills are Q&A ammunition.
7. Numbers aloud max 4: 27% fewer (or 57 vs 78), 16,560 missed screenings/yr, 46.5 vs 47.4 (translation alone doesn't work), €400k.
8. Close on the letter returning, now with the QR, + the ask in one sentence.
