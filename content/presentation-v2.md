# Silta

Same letter. Twenty points fewer come.

Junction 2026 · AstraZeneca — Equal Care Across Finland

Note:
Spine: people whose native language is not Finnish or Swedish attend cancer screening about 20 points less often, in all three programmes, and the gap is growing. It opens at the first step. Silta is a working, multilingual bridge across that step, safe to use today without anything from the county.

<p class="kicker">I · The gap</p>

## And it is growing

<div class="cols">
<div class="num"><span class="big">15.6 → 19.9</span><span>points: the cervical screening gap, 2020 → 2024. Breast went 19.9 → 22.4.</span></div>
<div class="num"><span class="big">≈16,560</span><span>screenings missed every year against equal attendance: breast 5,556, cervical 6,844, colorectal 4,161.</span></div>
<div class="num"><span class="big">×2</span><span>by 2035: 31,000–39,000 missed a year if the gap stays as it is, because the invited group keeps growing.</span></div>
</div>

<p class="src">Deep analysis §1, §6 (notes/2026-11-14-0145-silta-deep-data-analysis.md) · financial case (notes/2026-11-14-1630-silta-financial-lean-case.md). Breast and cervical widening is statistically significant; colorectal is flat.</p>

Note:
Women who skip cervical screening carry about 1.8 times the cervical cancer rate of those who attend (deep analysis §5). Do not say "lives saved".

<p class="kicker">III · At the door</p>

## Three things keep the door shut

<div class="cols">
<div class="card">
<h3>Language</h3>
<p>Omaolo, Maisa, Lunna and 116117 work in Finnish, Swedish and partly English, mostly behind bank ID. Non-native speakers are 4.8% of residents but 3.0% of digital-clinic users.</p>
<p class="fix">Silta: 14 languages, read aloud, no login.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Shame and modesty</h3>
<p>An intimate exam by a stranger. Symptoms you would not name to your own daughter. A recent gynaecological check-up is the strongest predictor of cervical screening among immigrant women.</p>
<p class="fix">Silta: tap instead of say, preferences sent ahead, a kit used at home.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Navigating the system</h3>
<p>Which door depends on your entitlement. Booking is a phone call with a callback, in Finnish. The right to a free interpreter exists, but most counties don't say how to ask.</p>
<p class="fix">Silta: the right door, a Finnish script, your rights and the clocks.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p class="lead" style="margin-top:0.8em">Once people are inside, follow-up is equal: colposcopy attendance is 87.1% vs 84.8%. <strong>The gap opens at the first step.</strong></p>

## She is already in the building

<div class="cols">
<div class="num"><span class="big lake">95%</span><span>of Somali-origin women had their latest doctor visit at a public health centre (about 20% of the general population).</span></div>

<div class="num"><span class="big">46.5% vs 47.4%</span><span>attendance with an invitation translated into the language of origin vs not (Norway RCT, n = 11,347): no effect.</span></div>
</div>

<p class="src">sources/2026-11-14-europepmc-unmet-need-and-access-by-origin.md (Maamu) · sources/2026-11-13-web-research-minorities-screening.md §B</p>

Note:
This is the turn of the story: translating letters does not work; acting when the person is already in front of a professional does. That is why Silta's main action is at the visit.

## From the letter to a kit offered in the room

<div class="cols five">
<div class="card lake"><p class="step">1 · Letter</p><h3>Understand it</h3><p>QR code on the invitation, or a photo of any letter, explained in her language.</p></div>

<p class="kicker">V · Trust</p>

## Compliance by design

<div class="cols four">
<div class="card"><p class="law">EU MDR Rule 11</p><h3>Not a medical device</h3><p>No diagnosis and no urgency rating. Route lanes come from clinician-reviewed content, never from the model. 112 on every screen.</p></div>
<div class="card"><p class="law">GDPR Art. 9</p><h3>No personal data</h3><p>No names, IDs, phones or emails. A random visit code, stored hashed, expires in 90 days and can be deleted.</p></div>
<div class="card"><p class="law">EU AI Act</p><h3>AI that asks first</h3><p>Gemini matches a person's words to a card and asks back. One tap by the person before anything changes.</p></div>
<div class="card"><p class="law">Patient Act §5</p><h3>Interpreter first</h3><p>The card names the language needed and the right to a free professional interpreter. Clinical phrases come from a vetted library.</p></div>
</div>

<p class="lead" style="margin-top:0.8em">Every published fact has a source and a verified-on date. Every admin change is audited and can be rolled back.</p>
